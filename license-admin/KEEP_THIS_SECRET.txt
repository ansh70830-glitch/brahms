bhrams_private_key.pem is the key that makes Bhrams licence keys.

- Keep it secret. Anyone who has it can make free licence keys.
- Back it up (password manager, USB drive). If you lose it, you can't make keys that existing copies of Bhrams accept.
- Never upload it to GitHub or send it to anyone. The .gitignore already leaves this folder out.
- Move this folder out of ~/Public: macOS can share that folder with other computers on your network.

To see the "Sell Bhrams" page in your packaged app, copy the .pem file to:
  ~/Library/Application Support/Bhrams/seller/bhrams_private_key.pem
